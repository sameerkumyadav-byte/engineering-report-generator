\section{Calculation}
The area of a rectangle is
\begin{equation}
A = L \times W
\end{equation}
Substituting the values:
\[
A = 5.0 \times 0.3 = 1.5 \ \mathrm{m^2}
\]


\section{Calculation}
The Perimeter of a rectangle is
\begin{equation}
A = 2 \times (L \times W)
\end{equation}
Substituting the values:
\[
A = 2 \times (5.0 \times 0.3) = 0.3 \ \mathrm{m^2}
\]
